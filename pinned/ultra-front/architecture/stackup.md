# Stackup

JLC 2-layer, 1.6 mm FR-4, 1 oz, HASL. Why 2 layers: < 100 mA DC, SPI at up
to 40 MHz over a few cm, no impedance control; a solid B.Cu GND pour under
the SPI run is enough. Top side carries every part (user-facing); the LCD
sits on the top side over a part-free area that routing may cross.
