# Stackup

Chosen: JLC2313_1.6 (2-layer, 1.6 mm FR-4, 1 oz, HASL; available: true). Matches requirements section 5.

Why 2 layers: 5 mA DC, no high-speed nets, no impedance control, 4 SMD parts plus one header. B.Cu GND pour (default) is enough. Class 2L. Controlled impedance: none (stack offers none). Board size is an output of placement (~15 x 12 mm soft target); needs are only J1 on an edge and C1 adjacent to J1. SMD top side only.
